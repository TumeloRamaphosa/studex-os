The platform

Your own desk, on subscription.

Counterparties run their trade on the same machinery we use. Each desk is a subscription, and buyers can sponsor seats for their suppliers.

  • Trading desk intelligence — prices, signals and market reads behind every quote.
  • Agent fleet — research, outreach, structuring and tracking agents working your pipeline.
  • Approval-gated actions — nothing external goes out without a human sign-off in-app.
  • Audit trail — every state change, approval and dispatch on an append-only record.

Settlement

USD in. USD out.

Buyers pay in USD by SWIFT transfer or in USDT. StudEx always receives USD — the platform never touches crypto.

Settlement runs on XREX Pay: deposit on order, balance on delivery, with a payment proof generated for your accounting, tax and regulatory file.

Compliance

Screened before the first quote.

Every counterparty passes sanctions and PEP screening at onboarding. XREX runs its own KYB review at first payment. No payments to or through sanctioned banks, ever.

Each deal closes with a trade documentation pack: invoice, payment proof, compliance screen record, permits and framework references.

Market access

Governments open doors. They don't sit at the table.

Governments are facilitators and market-access partners on this platform — never trade counterparties, and no payment ever flows to a state entity. Our framework registry tracks the agreements that make each corridor possible, and our first counterparty cohort — the Women's First 20, women-led African exporters — comes aboard through ministry-facilitated channels as design partners.
