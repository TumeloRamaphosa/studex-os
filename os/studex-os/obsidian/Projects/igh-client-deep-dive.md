## The Core Problem

**IGH has 875,000 TikTok followers but zero online booking infrastructure.**

The current website (`infinitegrowthhaven.com`) is a broken placeholder. When people click through from TikTok, they find:
- No service descriptions
- No pricing
- No booking capability
- No testimonials page
- No contact information
- Links that go nowhere

**Result:** Every TikTok viewer is a missed client. At even 1 conversion per 10,000 views, that's 87 lost bookings per video. At R550 average booking, that's **R47,850 in lost revenue per viral video.**

### The Siren Problem
The broken website is a "siren" — it draws traffic in (from TikTok, Google, Instagram) but converts nobody. It looks like a business. It has a URL. But it's broken. This is worse than having no website at all, because it creates false hope and wastes the traffic.

## Platform Strategy

### Why WhatsApp is the Centre of Gravity
South Africans overwhelmingly prefer WhatsApp for business communication. Any system that forces clients to a website or app will fail. The solution: **build the AI inside WhatsApp.**

```
WhatsApp AI Assistant (Base44 + OpenWA)
  ↓
24/7 booking, FAQ, payment link, confirmation
  ↓
Dr. Amelia only intervenes for complex queries
```

### Why a Website is Still Needed
- **Google SEO:** People search "spiritual healer Polokwane", "Ceragem therapy Johannesburg"
- **TikTok link in bio:** Must go somewhere professional
- **Credibility:** A proper website = real business
- **Instagram/Facebook:** All social links point to website
- **WhatsApp link:** The AI chatbot lives inside WhatsApp, but the website is the discovery layer

### Why Huly for Ops
| Tool Huly Replaces | What Huly Does |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp contact chaos | Centralised CRM with all client history |
| Excel scheduling | Visual calendar across 4 locations |
| Manual invoicing | Automated quotes and invoices |
| Email chains | Unified inbox per client |
| Trello for events | Project management for pop-up clinics |
| Notion docs | SOPs, training materials, protocols |

## The Fraud Problem (Urgent)

**IGH is currently vulnerable to payment fraud.** Scammers can:
1. Clone Dr. Amelia's TikTok account
2. Post fake booking instructions with a different payment number
3. Collect money from confused clients
4. Destroy trust in the IGH brand

**This is happening right now** to wellness practitioners across South Africa. The solution:
- All payments go through Dr. Amelia's official Paystack account
- Booking confirmation only fires after payment verification
- Anti-fraud notice on website: "We will NEVER ask you to pay to a different account"
- Base44 agent monitors social channels for cloned accounts
