## DETAILED SCORING BY DIMENSION

### 1. COMPANY SIZE (8/10)

**Evidence:**
- Founded 2013, operations in 60+ countries across Africa, Asia, Europe
- Estimated 200-500 employees (LinkedIn shows 300+ visible profiles)
- Cape Town HQ + offices in London, Singapore, Malaysia, Nigeria
-Acquired by South African media giant Naspers in 2017 (reported $50M+ deal)
- Series B funding from PayPal and Naspers Ventures
- Processing billions in crypto transactions annually

**Scoring Rationale:** Large enough to afford enterprise solutions but not so large that procurement is frozen. Naspers backing provides budget confidence.

---

### 2. TECH SOPHISTICATION (7/10)

**Evidence:**
- Modern fintech stack with microservices architecture
- Cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP) with global CDN
- Active use of AI/ML for:
  - Fraud detection and prevention
  - KYC/AML compliance automation
  - Trading algorithm optimization
  - Customer behavior analytics
- Strong engineering culture with published tech blog
- DevOps practices with CI/CD pipelines
- API-first architecture for partner integrations

**Scoring Rationale:** Sophisticated enough to appreciate StudeX OS value proposition. Likely already using multiple SaaS tools. Tech debt in legacy systems creates opportunity.

---

### 3. PAIN URGENCY (6/10)

**Evidence:**
- **Support Volume:** High transaction volumes = high support tickets; crypto volatility drives customer inquiries
- **Regulatory Pressure:** South Africa tightening crypto regulations; need for compliance automation
- **Expansion Capacity:** Growing into new markets requires scalable infrastructure
- **Churn Signals:** Competition intensifying from Binance, Valr, Crypto.com in SA market
- **Hiring Challenges:** Competing with global tech firms for engineering talent

**Scoring Rationale:** Moderate urgency — Luno is profitable and stable, but competitive pressure is increasing. Not a "burning platform" but clear improvement areas exist.
